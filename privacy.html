Privacy Policy

Five Widget · Last updated 19 November 2026

Five Widget (“Five”) is a photo-sharing app for close friends: the photos you send appear in the Five widget on your friends’ home screens, and each photo has its own chat. This page explains what the app collects, what it does with it, and who else sees it. Five is operated by Cheung Pak Wai, and “we” and “us” mean us.

What we collect

Your account. You sign in with Apple on iPhone or with Google on Android. We keep the identifier that Apple or Google gives us, the email address they confirm, and the name they send, if any. Five has no passwords.

Your profile. The nickname your friends see, and the first and last name you enter when you sign up.

Your friends. Your friends, your friend requests, the groups you are in and their names, and the invite links you create.

Photos you send. Each photo you send, a small preview of it, the caption you add, when it was taken, who it was sent to (one friend, a group, or all your friends) and who has seen it. Before a photo leaves your phone, the app saves it again as a new image, which drops its location and other camera details.

Chats under photos. The messages, replies and emoji reactions you send under a photo, and when they were sent.

Your purchases. A record of the credit you buy and your remaining balance. Purchases themselves are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.

Your devices. A notification token for each device, so we can tell your phone and your widget that a new photo has arrived.

Finding friends from your contacts

If you allow it, Five checks your contacts to find friends who already use it. The phone numbers and email addresses in your contacts are scrambled on your phone with a one-way hash (SHA-256) before they are sent, so we receive hashes, not the numbers or addresses themselves, and never names or any other contact details. We compare them with the hashes of Five users’ email addresses and phone numbers, and we do not keep the list.

One exception: if a contact card holds the email of someone on Five and a single phone number, we keep the hash of that number with their account, so that friends who know only their number can find them too. A phone number hash may also be added to your account when you join through an invite link that a friend made for your number. We never learn the number itself.

You can turn off contacts access at any time in your phone’s settings.

What we do with it

  • Deliver your photos to the friends and groups you choose, and show theirs in your widget and in the app.
  • Keep the chat under each photo.
  • Send notifications about new photos and messages.
  • Turn photos into Toons when you ask for it.
  • Process purchases and track your credit.

Who else sees it

A photo you turn into a Toon is sent to an AI provider. That is how Toons work: the photo is sent to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai), which passes it to the image model you picked in Settings, made by Google or OpenAI, and returns the illustration. We store only the illustration, not the photo you took. Photos you send without Toons are not sent to any AI service.

  • Your friends see your nickname, the photos you send them and the messages you write under those photos.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, notifications on iPhone, and in-app purchases.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, notifications on Android through Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Google Play purchases.
  • Amazon Web Services: our servers and the storage for the photos you send.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

What stays on your phone

  • Your photo diary. The calendar of your photos, and the feeds you have seen, are kept on your phone, so they stay after the photos are deleted from our servers.
  • Month recaps. A recap image is made on your phone and goes only wherever you choose to share it.
  • Your emoji rows are settings on your phone.

What we don’t do

Five contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party trackers. We do not profile you for advertising, and we do not sell data to anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Photos you send, their previews, and the chats and reactions under them are deleted from our servers 90 days after the photo is sent. Everything else is kept for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time; see Delete your account. It deletes your account, your photos and their files, your messages, your friends and your invite links straight away. The only thing kept is the record of your purchases, without your name or email, which we need for accounting.

Your choices

  • Camera, contacts and notification permissions can be turned off at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • You can change your nickname and name in the app.
  • You can unfriend someone or leave a group in the app.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing the address below.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Photos are served only to the people they were sent to, through our own servers. No service can promise perfect security, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise.

Children

Five is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or requests about your data: hello@fivewidget.com.
