Delete your account

Five Widget · Last updated 19 November 2026

You can delete your Five account, and the data that goes with it, from inside the app at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what goes and what stays.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Five and go to My Feed.
  2. Tap the account button at the top.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then confirm.

That is the whole process. It works the same on iPhone and Android. Deletion happens immediately; there is no waiting period, and it cannot be undone.

Already uninstalled the app? Email hello@fivewidget.com from the email address of your Apple or Google account, and we will delete it for you. You do not need to reinstall.

What is deleted

Immediately, and permanently, from our servers:

  • Your account: your name, nickname, email address, and the link to your Apple or Google sign-in.
  • Every photo you sent, with its preview and caption. The image files are removed from storage, not just unlinked.
  • Your messages and emoji reactions under any photo, yours or a friend’s.
  • Your friends, friend requests, group memberships and invite links. Groups you created carry on for their other members.
  • Every signed-in session, and the notification registration for every device you used.

Photos already shown on your friends’ phones may stay there, in their widget and their photo diary, because those copies are on their devices.

What is kept, and why

  • Purchase records: what was bought, when, and on which store, with no name or email attached. We need these for accounting and for refunds.

Unused credit is lost. It has no cash value and is not refundable; see the Terms of Use.

The photo diary and the feeds saved on the phone you delete from are cleared too, so save any photos you want to keep before you delete.
